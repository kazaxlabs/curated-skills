# Unit Economics

Definitions and formulas for the metrics named in `SKILL.md`. Use the same period for numerator and denominator, and
state the period and N next to every result. This file sets no targets: compare against the business's own history
or a source opened and cited at the time.

## Customer acquisition cost (CAC)

```
CAC = (marketing spend + sales costs attributable to acquisition) / new customers won
```

- Same period for spend and customers; with long sales cycles, lag the customers by the typical cycle length.
- Blended CAC (all channels) hides differences; compute per channel when spend can be attributed.

## Gross margin per customer or per job

```
Gross margin = revenue − direct costs (materials, direct labour, subcontracting, processing fees)
Gross margin % = gross margin / revenue
```

## Lifetime value (LTV)

For repeat or recurring customers:

```
LTV = average gross margin per period × expected number of periods a customer stays
Expected periods ≈ 1 / churn rate per period (only when churn is roughly constant)
```

For one-off jobs with referrals or repeat work, use observed history instead: average gross margin per customer over
[N] years, including repeat jobs, for customers acquired in [period].

## Payback period

```
Payback (months) = CAC / gross margin per customer per month
```

For one-off jobs: compare CAC with the gross margin of the first job.

## LTV to CAC ratio

```
LTV / CAC
```

A ratio means nothing without its inputs' periods and N. Do not quote a "healthy ratio" without an opened source.

## Efficiency metrics (growth companies)

```
Burn multiple = net cash burned / net new recurring revenue (same period)
Runway (months) = cash available / average monthly net cash outflow
```
