We run a booking and appointment-reminder SaaS for independent physiotherapy and massage clinics in Quebec. Everyone pays a flat $49 a month for unlimited practitioners and unlimited SMS reminders. We have 85 clinics. Most have 1 to 3 practitioners, but a handful of multi-location clinics have 15 or more practitioners and send thousands of SMS a month, which costs us real money in SMS fees. Competitors charge between $30 and $60 per practitioner per month. Churn is about 1% a month and almost every free trial converts to paid.

How should we restructure our pricing?
