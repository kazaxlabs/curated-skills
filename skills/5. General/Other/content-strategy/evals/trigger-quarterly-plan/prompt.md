I run marketing, alone, for a B2B SaaS that sells online booking and invoicing software to independent dog groomers in Canada and the US. We have about 300 paying customers. The goal for content is free-trial signups.

What I know:
- Questions prospects ask on demo calls: how to cut no-shows, whether clients can book from Instagram, how to take deposits, how to price a full groom versus a bath.
- Objections: "my clients are used to texting me", "I'm not techy", "I already use one of the big salon booking apps".
- Support tickets: setting up deposits, reminder texts, moving clients over from a paper appointment book.
- Current blog: 14 posts with no real plan behind them. The only one with steady traffic is "How much should I charge for a dog groom?".
- Competitors: two big general booking apps for salons and spas. Neither publishes anything specific to groomers.
- Resources: me, one post a week, no video.

Plan our content for the next quarter: which topics we should own, what to publish first, and why. I've given you everything I have, so please give me the plan rather than a questionnaire.
