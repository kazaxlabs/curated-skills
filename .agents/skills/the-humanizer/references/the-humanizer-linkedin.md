# The Humanizer — LinkedIn Review Guide (Standalone Reference)

## LinkedIn-Specific Markers

### Phrase-Level
- LinkedIn pivot transitions: "But here's the thing", "And here's the kicker", "Here's what most people miss", "Let me explain", "Here's why that matters"
- Engagement bait closers: "Agree?", "Thoughts?", "What would you add?", "Drop a comment if you've experienced this", "Repost if this resonates"
- Vulnerability performance phrases: "I'll be honest", "Can I be real for a second?", "I'll be vulnerable here", "I wasn't going to share this but..."
- Fake humility: "I'm no expert, but...", "I don't have all the answers, but...", "This might be controversial, but..."
- Tag-and-thank: tagging 5+ people at the end with "Shoutout to..."
- Dream-realized language: "I realized my dream", "A dream come true", "Pinch me moment"
- Arrow chain format: using → arrows to show a process/flow
- ALL-CAPS single-word injection: capitalizing individual words mid-sentence (e.g. "We've onboarded HUNDREDS of customers.")
- "What if I told you..." curiosity hook: recognized ghostwriter/AI template
- "Here's what nobody tells you about..." insider framing: formula used without actual insider knowledge
- "Read that again." / "Let that sink in." permission phrases: dropped after unremarkable observations to manufacture gravity
- "And honestly?" fake candor opener: dropped before non-controversial claims

### Structural Markers
- One-line paragraph formatting: every sentence as its own paragraph (LinkedIn's #1 AI tell)
- Hook > 3-point list > mic-drop closer template
- Explaining the algorithm: telling people why to comment or share ("Gets it into more feeds")
- Vulnerability bait hook: opening with personal failure then pivoting to tidy lesson
- "We didn't just build X. We built Y" negation upgrade
- Hyperbole opener: "X will never be the same." or "Everything changed."
- Common-belief-then-counter opener: states common belief, attributes to "most people", knocks it down
- Period-separated word emphasis: "every. single. day." or "This. Is. The. Moment."
- Self-intro paragraph at post bottom: formal credentials dumped at the end
- Information-withheld hook: omitting subject to force "...more" click
- "X is [positive]. [X variant] is a whole different game" contrast formula
- Cliché proverb opener: "Work smarter, not harder"
- External link CTA ending: link in body or "link in comments" penalizes reach by ~60%
- Achievement post formula: announcement + team thanks + generic lesson + emoji sign-off
- Fake dialogue/conversation format: fabricated role dialogue (CEO/CMO, Founder/Investor)

## Hook vs. Value Calibration

### Stage 1 — Distribution (first 30-60 minutes)
Hook quality determines whether the algorithm distributes broadly by forcing a "see more" click.

### Stage 2 — Continued Distribution (ongoing)
Dwell time, saves, and substantive comments determine continuous distribution.
- One save = 5x reach value of a like.
- Substantive comments = 2.4x reach boost vs. surface reactions.

### Hooks that Clear Stage 1 AND Earn Stage 2:
1. Specific consequence opener (named result, not lesson)
2. Data point with personal stakes (one number + personal stakes)
3. Contrarian claim with named evidence
4. Story that ends unresolved (real situation, no tidy lesson)

## LinkedIn Scoring Dimensions
- **AI-Likeness** (1–10, target 1–3)
- **Authenticity** (1–10, target 8–10)
- **Reader Value** (1–10, target 7–10)
- **Domain Credibility** (1–10, target 7–10)

## LinkedIn Rewrite Rules
- Keep under 1,300 chars (short-form) or 3,000 chars (long-form).
- Don't stack hashtags at the bottom; weave 1-3 naturally or drop them.
- Remove engagement bait closers entirely.
- Replace arrow-chains with real sentences.
- Group one-line sentences into real 2-4 sentence paragraphs.
- Remove decorative emoji.
